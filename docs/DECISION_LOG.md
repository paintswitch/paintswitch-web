# PaintSwitch decision log

Last updated: 2026-08-01

This log records material approved decisions. Dates are approval dates when supplied; otherwise the entry identifies when the decision was first documented as confirmed. Preserve superseded entries and link their replacements.

## D-001 — Standalone PaintSwitch brand

- **Date:** 2026-07-11
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** PaintSwitch is a standalone consumer brand completely separate from Jen Contracting. Never use Jen Contracting branding, “powered by Jen,” shared identity, or any customer-facing connection.
- **Reason:** Establishes the governing brand boundary and prevents customer confusion.
- **Consequences:** All customer-facing identity, copy, metadata, experiences, and future integrations must present PaintSwitch independently.
- **Supersedes:** Any prior undocumented assumption that PaintSwitch could be customer-facing as part of, shared with, or powered by Jen Contracting. No prior decision ID is documented.

## D-002 — DMV launch market

- **Date:** 2026-07-11
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Launch in the DMV. Exact cities, counties, ZIP codes, travel zones, and service boundaries remain TBD.
- **Reason:** Establishes the regional launch focus without inventing unsupported coverage details.
- **Consequences:** Product and marketing may refer to the DMV, but address eligibility and travel rules cannot be enforced or promised until approved.
- **Supersedes:** None documented.

## D-003 — Technology-driven mainstream positioning

- **Date:** 2026-07-17
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Position PaintSwitch as mid-market, convenience-first, technology-first, and technology-driven mainstream, not as an extension of Jen Contracting's luxury positioning.
- **Reason:** Aligns the brand with a clear, accessible, technology-enabled buying experience.
- **Consequences:** Product and messaging should emphasize convenience, clarity, speed, and mainstream accessibility without inheriting Jen Contracting positioning.
- **Supersedes:** Any prior working assumption that PaintSwitch should inherit Jen Contracting's luxury positioning. No prior decision ID is documented.

## D-005 — Hybrid labor model

- **Date:** 2026-07-17
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Labor uses a hybrid model of employee and subcontractor crews.
- **Reason:** Establishes the approved high-level workforce model.
- **Consequences:** Future operating, scheduling, quality, compliance, and customer-experience requirements must support both crew types without making unapproved promises about assignment.
- **Supersedes:** None documented.

## D-006 — Four marketed services with controlled sales lanes

- **Date:** 2026-07-29 for marketed services and sales-lane limits. Residential interior as the operational V1 is Confirmed; its exact earlier historical source date remains uncertain and the rule was reaffirmed on 2026-08-01.
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Residential interior painting is the operational V1 for instant firm pricing and checkout. Market interior, exterior, cabinet, and commercial painting at launch; restrict instant firm pricing and checkout to qualifying standard residential interiors; require photo review for complex interiors and custom consultation for exterior, cabinet, and commercial work.
- **Reason:** Maintains broad market visibility while limiting automated firm pricing to the project class approved for safe standardization.
- **Consequences:** Product eligibility must route customers by service and complexity before a firm price is shown. The current standalone Drywall Repair service card is not approval of an additional marketed category and requires clarification.
- **Supersedes:** Any working assumption that every marketed service can receive instant firm pricing. No prior decision ID is documented.

## D-007 — Room-by-room pricing

- **Date:** 2026-07-29
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Calculate qualifying interior pricing room by room.
- **Reason:** Makes each room's scope explicit and creates a consistent calculation unit.
- **Consequences:** Quote intake and future pricing logic must preserve room-level inputs and calculate the project from those room-level results.
- **Supersedes:** Any undocumented whole-project-only pricing assumption. No prior decision ID is documented.

## D-008 — Room-size tiers

- **Date:** 2026-07-30
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Room square footage equals length multiplied by width. Small is 120 sq ft or less; Medium is 121–200 sq ft; Large is 201–300 sq ft; Oversized is more than 300 sq ft and requires dimensions and photo review.
- **Reason:** Defines approved, testable size classifications for room-by-room quoting.
- **Consequences:** Boundary handling must be exact. Oversized rooms cannot remain in unsupported automated firm pricing.
- **Supersedes:** Any prior undocumented room-size bands. No prior decision ID is documented.

## D-009 — Required quote inputs

- **Date:** 2026-07-31 (documented as confirmed; earlier approval date not provided)
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Quote inputs include room type and size, walls, ceilings, baseboards/trim/crown, doors, closets, ceiling height, repairs, color changes, and occupied/furniture level.
- **Reason:** Establishes the minimum categories of project information needed for the approved room-based model.
- **Consequences:** Future quoting must represent these inputs at room level where applicable. Exact choices, thresholds, and pricing effects remain TBD unless separately confirmed.
- **Supersedes:** None documented.

## D-010 — Ceiling pricing remains unapproved

- **Date:** 2026-07-31 (documented as confirmed; earlier approval date not provided)
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Exact ceiling-height tiers and surcharges are not approved.
- **Reason:** Prevents working assumptions from becoming unsupported live pricing.
- **Consequences:** Ceiling height must be collected, but no firm-price rule based on an unapproved tier, multiplier, or surcharge may be used.
- **Supersedes:** Any unapproved ceiling-tier or surcharge assumption. No prior decision ID is documented.

## D-011 — No-call qualifying sale and online booking

- **Date:** 2026-07-11 for quick quotes and closing qualifying sales before a required call; 2026-07-17 for instant pricing and online booking
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** The launch model includes instant pricing and online booking, with the goal of providing quick quotes and closing qualifying sales online before anyone needs to call the customer.
- **Reason:** Implements PaintSwitch's convenience-first, technology-first positioning.
- **Consequences:** The qualifying standard-interior journey must be capable of reaching firm price, checkout, and booking without a mandatory pre-sale call. Exact checkout, deposit, and scheduling rules remain TBD or Proposed.
- **Supersedes:** Any working assumption that a phone call is mandatory before every qualifying sale. No prior decision ID is documented.

## D-013 — Flat fuel/materials adjustment

- **Date:** 2026-07-16
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Apply one flat $125 fuel/materials adjustment to every project.
- **Reason:** Establishes the approved project-level fuel/materials adjustment.
- **Consequences:** Every project price must include the adjustment exactly once. It is separate from unapproved base prices, travel-zone adjustments, ceiling surcharges, repair allowances, coat rules, minimums, deposits, and cancellation policies.
- **Supersedes:** The incomplete 2026-07-31 documentation statement that no numeric pricing value was confirmed. No prior decision ID documented the $125 adjustment.
